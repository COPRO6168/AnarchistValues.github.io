## Organisation

### Einheit

- An anarchist organization should strive for programmatic unity in order to be more effective.
- A diversity of strategies and tactics within an organization is more important than unity within it.
- Anarchists should come together in a unified organization based on voluntary discipline and clear structure.
- A broad synthesis of different ideas and approaches promotes diversity and allows an organization to grow.
- All members of an anarchist organization should follow a common, collectively agreed-upon political line.

### Spezifizität

- Revolutionary anarchists should organize themselves within both anarchist membership organizations and mass movements.
- A strong unified organization that brings together all struggles is more effective than separate revolutionary and mass organizations.
- Both a political organization and mass organizations are needed to advance the social revolution.
- Anarchists should be part of a single revolutionary organization rather than working outside of it.
- Specifically anarchist organizations are necessary because mass organizations can lose their revolutionary potential.

### Föderalismus

- Decentralized, autonomous groups are more effective than centrally coordinated structures.
- Without a coordinated strategy, the anarchist struggle remains fragmented and ineffective.
- The autonomy of individual collectives must not be restricted by higher-level structures.
- Anarchist organizations must be prefigurative and should therefore not allow hierarchies.
- Self-governance is effective only when it is coordinated with other groups and shaped collectively.

## Theorie

### Philosophie

- Consciousness determines existence.
- Social change arises primarily from new ideas and values.
- Material relations of production are the central driving force of human history.
- Individual morality and ethics are the main driving forces of social change.
- The development of technology and the economy determines the ideologies within society.

### Methodik

- The best way to understand a society is to analyze its internal contradictions and trends.
- Analyses of society should primarily be based on sociological statistics and observable phenomena.
- Individual social problems can be identified and solved in isolation based on measurable data.
- If a political analysis cannot be refuted or confirmed using concrete data, it is not a valid basis.
- Social developments are not linear or predictable; rather, they are constantly in a state of flux due to their internal contradictions.

### Subjekt

- Humanity is primarily divided along lines of gender, nationality, ethnicity, religion, and other identities.
- A socially just society can only be achieved through the recognition and promotion of specific identities.
- The defining characteristic of a society’s exploited class is its economic leverage for change.
- Discrimination and marginalization can be understood and combated independently of the economic base.
- The consistent dismantling of class society automatically leads to the abolition of all forms of oppression.

## Praxis

### Radikalität

- Capitalism can be overcome through gradual reforms without a radical upheaval of the existing order.
- Cooperatives and the petty bourgeoisie (retailers, artists, freelancers) are revolutionary partners from an economic perspective.
- It is worthwhile to improve the conditions of the struggle through parliamentary participation and elections.
- A left-wing government is preferable to a right-wing government because it can also implement positive changes.
- Reforms enforced through pressure from the masses stabilize the system rather than challenge it.

### Militanz

- Revolutionary violence, such as the guillotine, is unacceptable, no matter how evil a person may be.
- Oppressed groups have the right to defend themselves against violence with counter-violence.
- Any form of violence can inevitably lead to new forms of domination and should therefore be rejected.
- Revolutionary movements must be prepared to resist repression by any means necessary, even if those means end bloody.
- A liberated society cannot be created through violence, but only by building nonviolent structures.

### Fokus

- Counterpower arises primarily through long-term organizing in the workplace, in the neighborhood, or in universities.
- Self-organizing in everyday life empowers people more than large, high-profile protests or campaigns.
- Without spectacular, high-profile actions, a movement will fade away, no matter how self-organized it is.
- Campaigning on specific issues can mobilize more people than grassroots work in everyday life.
- Large, media-friendly campaigns quickly fizzle out if they aren’t backed by grassroots structures that have grown over the years.

## Gesellschaft

### Geschlechter

- Only the abolition of gender will truly achieve equality between trans and cis people.
- Sexual freedom goes hand in hand with the abolition of both normative and queer identities.
- Women’s liberation will be complete once the category “woman” and gendered language, culture, and infrastructure no longer exist.
- Genders will always exist, which is why equality and recognition of all genders are essential.
- Biological foundations give rise to social gender characteristics that must be actively combated or regulated.

### Fortschritt

- The development of the productive forces is necessary to minimize strenuous and time-consuming work and to create more time for creativity.
- Humans should not play God. Genetic engineering and interference with evolution must be rejected.
- The increasing technologicalization of humanity alienates people from nature and should therefore be curbed.
- The scarcity of resources on Earth necessitates that, over time, humans explore and colonize other planets.
- Artificial intelligence, robotics, and automation are useful technologies for a highly complex society.

### Struktur

- Standards and uniformity are useful and necessary for organizing complex production chains.
- In a liberated society, rules are still needed that can lead to sanctions or penalties in the event of violations.
- A certain portion of production must be planned centrally and for the long term, by trained experts.
- In an anarchist society, everyone should have the opportunity to express their opinion on every decision.
- Anarchy means that there is no longer any form of authority—not even mayors, chairpersons, or other officials.
